# Reporting a vulnerability

Email **framewitness@mail.getcertsprint.com** with "Security" in the subject.
That is the public support address for Frame Witness.

Include the affected version, what you did, and what came back. If you need to
send anything sensitive, say so first and we will agree on a channel.

You will get an acknowledgement within three working days and an assessment
within ten.

## Scope

In scope: the Frame Witness application, its website and account service, and
this repository.

Out of scope: output pasted from automated scanners without a demonstrated
impact, and anything requiring a victim to be running malware already.

Do not test against systems or data you do not own.

## What we ask

Give us a chance to ship a fix before you publish. We will credit you when the
fix goes out unless you would rather stay anonymous.

There is no bug bounty.
